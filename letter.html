💌 A letter from Poko

Gumi,
let me tell you what this first month feels like.

Gumi, I have tried to think of a perfect way to write this. The truth is, I don't think there is one. Because what I feel for you is made from a hundred tiny things rather than one giant sentence.

It started in the weirdest possible way. You came onto AirTalk and started with, “pata hai aaj kya hua?” You had a crazy story, the internet kept cutting you off, I kept saying I couldn't hear you, and somehow that mess ended with us on Google Meet talking for hours.

And I remember that feeling. The conversation didn't feel forced. I didn't have to keep thinking of what to say next. We just kept talking. Then it was 4 AM. Then there was another conversation. Then another. And without either of us really planning it, you started becoming someone I looked forward to.

Then came your childhood pictures. Then the real picture. I still remember thinking you looked really cute, but I didn't want to ruin something that felt so new by saying too much too soon. So I kept it to myself for a while.

Then “Gumnam” became Gumi. And somehow I became Poko. I don't know why a silly nickname can feel this personal, but it does. When you call me Poko, it feels like you are calling your version of me.

I know the beginning wasn't completely easy. Trust wasn't automatic. I know you had reasons to be careful. I know there were things from before that made you scared of trusting someone again.

And I don't want to punish you for being careful. I would rather earn trust properly than demand it.

And yes, there were moments that hurt me too. Sometimes when something happens, my stupid brain immediately asks, “Did I do something wrong? Am I not enough? Does she trust me?” I know that's not always fair to you. I'm learning that loving someone doesn't mean turning every difficult moment into proof that something is wrong with me.

What I do know is that I care about you deeply.

I love your random videos. I love when you sing. I love the way you care about me. I love when you ask if I've eaten. I love when you worry because I sound a little sad. I love when you call me cute. I love your “mera bacha”. I love your kisses. And yes, I am still GreedyMon. 😭

But more than all the cute things, I love the fact that you feel like a real person to me. You have your moods, your bad days, your fears, your habits I don't understand, your nail-biting that I absolutely do not approve of, your friends, your own life, your own way of seeing things. I don't want some imaginary perfect version of you. I want to know you.

“ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਕੋਈ perfect promise ਨਹੀਂ ਦੇਣਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦਾ। ਮੈਂ ਬਸ ਇਹ ਕਹਿਣਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ ਕਿ ਜਦੋਂ ਚੀਜ਼ਾਂ ਔਖੀਆਂ ਹੋਣ, ਮੈਂ ਭੱਜਣਾ ਨਹੀਂ ਚਾਹੁੰਦਾ। ਜਦੋਂ ਤੂੰ ਰੋਵੇਂ, ਮੈਂ ਤੈਨੂੰ ਸਮਝਣ ਦੀ ਕੋਸ਼ਿਸ਼ ਕਰਨਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ। ਜਦੋਂ ਤੂੰ ਖੁਸ਼ ਹੋਵੇਂ, ਮੈਂ ਤੇਰੀ ਖੁਸ਼ੀ ਵਿੱਚ ਖੁਸ਼ ਹੋਣਾ ਚਾਹੁੰਦਾ ਹਾਂ।”

I don't know what the future will look like. Nobody honestly does after one month. But I know I am very grateful that this first chapter exists. I am grateful for that random AirTalk conversation. I'm grateful for the stupid internet problems. I'm grateful for the lizard story. I'm grateful for Google Meet. I'm grateful for Gumi. And I'm very, very grateful that somewhere in all of that, I became Poko.

So happy first monthiversary, meri Gumi.

“ਅਜੇ ਤਾਂ ਸਿਰਫ਼ ਇੱਕ ਮਹੀਨਾ ਹੋਇਆ ਏ… ਪਰ ਤੂੰ ਮੇਰੇ ਦਿਲ ਵਿੱਚ ਜਿੰਨੀ ਜਗ੍ਹਾ ਬਣਾ ਲਈ ਏ, ਉਹ ਗਿਣਤੀ ਨਾਲ ਨਹੀਂ ਦੱਸੀ ਜਾ ਸਕਦੀ।”

And if you ever forget how special you are to me, I'll probably annoy you until you remember. 😭❤️

— Poko
