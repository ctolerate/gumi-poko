01 · OUR STORY

How a random conversation
became my favourite person.

I could write a clean, perfect version of the story. But ours wasn't clean or planned. It was messy internet, random jokes, late-night conversations and two people who just… clicked.

“Pata hai aaj kya hua?”

That was the opening. She had a crazy day and a lizard had apparently decided her head was the perfect landing spot. 😭

The internet had other plans.

Three or four times the connection ruined the story. I kept telling her I couldn't hear her. Eventually she suggested Zoom/Google Meet — and somehow that made everything better.

And then we talked. A lot.

Where are you from? What do you like? Random things. Food. Spicy food. Life. And suddenly it was 4 AM.

“Gumnam.” → Gumi.

She didn't simply hand me her name. I had to pull it out of “gumnam”. And later she gave me mine: Poko. Somehow those names stuck harder than our real ones.

The accidental Google Meet reveal.

My real name appeared through Gmail before I planned to tell her. My reaction was basically: “arre yaar 😭 I was going to tell you later!”

Then came trust.

It wasn't instant. She had reasons to be careful. I understood that. And when she eventually trusted me enough to share her number, I knew that tiny thing meant more than just having a contact saved.

And then… us. ❤️

Things moved quickly. Maybe very quickly. But somewhere between the calls, the long texts, the care, the songs, the random videos and the “khana kha liya, Poko?” — we became something real to each other.

“The weirdest part?
I wasn't looking for you.
And then you happened.”

The first photo. The childhood photos. The later real photo that made Poko quietly think, “yaar… she's really cute.” Not everything had to be said immediately. Sometimes you just know you want to keep talking.

“ਤੇ ਫਿਰ ਗੱਲਾਂ ਗੱਲਾਂ ਵਿੱਚ… ਤੂੰ ਮੇਰੀ favourite ਗੱਲ ਬਣ ਗਈ।”
